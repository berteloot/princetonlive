Parking

Princeton, NJ parking rules and meter hours.

No overnight parking on any former Princeton Borough street, 2 to 6 am. Not all streets carry a sign saying so. A home with no driveway can buy one on-street permit from the Clerk's Office, $30 a quarter, valid 6 pm to 9 am on your own street. Downtown meters are payable monday to thursday 9 am to 8 pm, friday and saturday 9 am to 9 pm, sunday 1 pm to 8 pm.

The overnight ban

No overnight parking on any former Princeton Borough street, 2 to 6 am. It applies to every former Borough street overnight, and the municipality notes that not all streets carry a sign saying so. A home with no driveway can buy one on-street permit from the Clerk's Office, $30 a quarter, valid 6 pm to 9 am on your own street. Check the sign, or check the official page, before leaving a car out.

The 90-minute pay stations start at 10 am. The 15-minute Wawa meters are payable all day, every day.

Meter rates

Thirty-minute spaces cost $1.00 and 90-minute zones $3.00. Rates rise on September 14, 2026, to $1.25 and $3.50.

Before an evening downtown

Meters run into the evening, so a show at the Garden Theatre usually falls inside paid hours. Princeton Public Library cardholders can validate a Spring Street Garage ticket at the Sands Library Building; see library benefits.

How current this is

These rules were read off the municipal parking page on August 18, 2026. Parking rules change and carry fines, so confirm on the official page before you rely on them. Residential permit costs and applications are not published on the municipal parking page, so PrincetonLive does not list them.
