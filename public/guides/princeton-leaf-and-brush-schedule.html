Yard waste

Princeton leaf, brush and log collection schedule.

Princeton splits the municipality into five collection sections, and your section decides when branches, loose leaves and bagged leaves are picked up. This page lists the 2026 dates for all five sections and the streets in each one.

How yard waste collection works

Leaf, branch, and log collection begins on the listed section date. Put material out by 7 AM on the start date, no more than 7 days prior.

Find your section by street below, or use the garbage schedule by street, which lists the section alongside the weekly collection day.
